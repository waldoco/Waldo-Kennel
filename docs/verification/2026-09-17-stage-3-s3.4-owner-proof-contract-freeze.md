# Stage 3 S3.4 owner-proof contract freeze

Baseline: S3.2 at `6a34ef98b67fbe8a951e9aabd94f3e7365c56f9a`.

S3.4 extends S1 with a separate short-lived, one-time owner proof. The app-run owner authority authenticates proof minting; the owner bearer never enters the proof, adapter, renderer, persistence or logs. A proof binds its verifier to app run, mission, exact content SHA-256, exact target identity, target/question generation, closed command class and expiry. Verification consumes the proof atomically. Replay, expiry, wrong tuple and concurrent consumption fail closed.

Closed classes are `turn`, `steer`, `answer`, `interrupt`, `cancel`, `replace`, `approval`, and `accept`. Routine current-generation continuation classes are turn, steer, answer, interrupt and cancel. They require exact one-time owner proof but no native material dialog. Replacement, approval and Result Accept are material transitions and require an exact native confirmation reference in addition to the owner proof. A proof carries owner authority only for its exact tuple; adapter authentication remains separate transport identity.

S3.4 implements the proof kernel and durable consumption fence first. Integration with adapter ingress is blocked on the frozen S3.3 transport surface and must not be guessed. Existing governed-command dispatch or Result Acceptance call sites are not rewired until that dependency exists and exact target mappings are reviewed.
