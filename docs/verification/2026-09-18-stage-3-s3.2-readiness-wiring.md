## Review-blocker fixes in this slice (adversarial review round on ac38aa8f)

- HIGH-2 (pre-evaluation persistence): `runPlanningTurn` now evaluates the strict envelope BEFORE any planner turn is persisted. The durable planner turn carries the EVALUATED canonical packet (derived status, re-keyed issues) as a `domain.PlanningEvaluatedReply` (packet + snapshot-bound fence), with the turn kind derived from the evaluated status and the post-evaluation wait state (`owner` or `system`) written in the same transaction as the turn (`AppendPlanningProviderTurn` takes the target wait state; `SetPlanningSessionWaitingSystem` is removed). A planner "ready" the evaluator blocks is durably `readiness_blocked`, never `plan_proposal`, and no Plan exists for it. A failed evaluation persists no turn; the session returns to the owner with a typed failure (`PLANNING_READINESS_EVALUATION_FAILED` or the typed apierr code) so a retry is a fresh turn.
- MEDIUM (replay idempotency): `resumePlanningReply` replays the durable evaluated reply verbatim - no fresh snapshot, no re-keyed packet, no Plan attempted under the old request identity. Replays of needs_context/blocked turns surface the stored packet (previously dropped); replays of proposal turns no longer re-run `finishPlanningEvaluation` (deleted; `evaluatePlanningReply` now only feeds the live path).
- Compatibility: `domain.DecodePlanningEvaluatedReply` also reads pre-fix raw-envelope payloads as claim-only replies with no fence, replayed verbatim. Explicit compat path, not a silent fallback; flagged here for review.
