# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Waldo Kennel should be a safe, constructive place to build and discuss
governed agent software. Contributors, maintainers and community members are
expected to participate without harassment, intimidation or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, repository boundaries and disclosed authority.
- Welcome questions and make room for people with different levels of context.
- Correct mistakes openly and avoid overstating what software or tests prove.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized
language, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption,
and publishing credentials, private source, transcripts or vulnerability details.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
community events and private project communications. It also applies when a
person is publicly representing Waldo or acting with repository authority.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `woof@heywaldo.in`. Include links or other
evidence, the impact, and any immediate safety concern. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the
private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible,
disclose conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. Responses may
include clarification, content removal, a warning, temporary participation
limits, or a permanent ban. A maintainer named in a report must not adjudicate
that report alone.

Good-faith reports and appeals will not result in retaliation. Deliberately
false or abusive reports may themselves violate this policy.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
